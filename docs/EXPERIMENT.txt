CONCEPT8 / VETTING HARBOR — VALIDATION EXPERIMENT
Status: prepared, not executed. All demo records are synthetic.

HYPOTHESIS
A marketplace operator with actual listing/install access, or a procurement team owning a purchase gate, will agree to use version-specific evidence in a real decision. An independent assessor can preserve control over findings despite funding incentives.

FIRST COMMITMENT
Invite one actual marketplace and several real buyers to inspect manually prepared evidence packs for two consenting agents. Name the operator, decision owner, budget owner, review criteria and accepted evidence use before work begins. Select one of the five proposed modules for the initial pilot; keep all five as separate offer tests.

STUDY PROTOCOL
1. Record documented publisher consent and scoped sandbox/test authority. Do not use public-form uploads for private contracts, logs or secrets.
2. Record exact versions, manifests, sources, protocol/environment and expected/observed results. Keep failed/inconclusive outcomes.
3. Agree issuer qualifications, payer disclosure, conflict declarations, recusal, no outcome-contingent fees and challenge/correction handling.
4. Prepare manual packs for two agents and observe both operator and procurement reviewers using them in a real workflow.
5. Ask how the evidence affected listing/install/purchase decisions. Record actual commitment and attribution; do not count synthetic walkthroughs as demand proof.

GO
Credible marketplace distribution pilot OR procurement team formally agreeing to use the report in a purchase gate; named accepted criteria; attributable consented artifacts; independence policy established; ideally funded evaluation.

STOP / REVISE
No gatekeeper access; no agreed evidence acceptance standard; assessor independence cannot be established; evidence does not affect the purchase decision. Do not assume two-sided network effects.

COMMERCIAL TESTS
$2,500 per reviewed listing study OR $1,500/month marketplace pilot are test hypotheses. No accepted pricing or payments have been demonstrated. Discuss scope and payer conflicts explicitly; there is no active checkout.

ACQUISITION
Marketplace operators and publisher ecosystems first, via ecosystem partners. Procurement/GRC workshops second. Verify distribution access before broad acquisition.
No ads or outreach were sent in this build.
